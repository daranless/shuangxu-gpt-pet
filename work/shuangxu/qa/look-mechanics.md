# Natural gaze mechanics

Feet, lower dress, tail and torso stay anchored in the same frontal standing pose. Eyes lead, then the head pitches or yaws slightly at the neck. Horns and hair follow the head. Preserve the screen-right rose hair ornament throughout; never swap it to the opposite side. Wings remain attached and broad silhouette stays stable. No whole-character rotation or turntable. Face remains visible at every direction.

Up: chin lifted, both pupils visibly toward upper eyelids, nose directed upward. Down: chin lowered, both pupils visibly toward lower eyelids, lowered eyebrows/upper eyelids indicate downward attention. Right and left: nose and pupils shift consistently toward the viewer's respective edge through mild head yaw, while torso and feet stay frontal. Diagonals combine horizontal yaw with vertical pitch in gradual even steps. The pet's existing lavender eyes, pupils, sclera and eyelids move as coherent facial features.
